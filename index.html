The Vibe

A rooftop stage,
three floors up.

Music Cafe sits on the third floor of Acharya College Road — part cafe, part stage. By day it's coffee, brunch and laptops; by evening the string lights come on, the guitars come out, and the rooftop turns into Chikkabanavara's living room.

It's the spot students and neighbours already pack out on weekends — with 1,463 ratings on Justdial and a 4.5 rating on Places to show for it. What it has never had is a home on the web of its own: until now, a single Facebook page was the whole story.

This page gives the venue what the music deserves — the week's line-up, the menu, the gallery and a one-tap table booking, all in one place.
